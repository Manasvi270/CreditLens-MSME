# CreditLens-MSME Risk Model Card

## 📌 Model Overview
- **Model Name**: Calibrated MSME Credit Risk Scorecard
- **Model Version**: `v1.0.0-logistic-scorecard`
- **Model Type**: Calibrated Logistic Regression (Platt Sigmoid Calibration)
- **Challenger Model**: XGBoost Classifier (`v1.0.0-xgb-challenger`)
- **Developer**: CreditLens-MSME Engineering Team

---

## 🎯 Purpose & Intended Use
- **Purpose**: To provide transparent, calibrated Probability of Default (PD) predictions for Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) based on traditional and alternative financial data.
- **Intended Users**: Credit Officers, Risk Analysts, NBFC/Bank Underwriters, Compliance Officers.
- **Intended Use**: Decision-support tool for evaluating MSME loan application risk, setting risk bands, identifying key risk drivers, and exploring restructured loan terms.
- **Non-Intended Use**: 
  - **NOT** an autonomous loan approval or rejection system.
  - **NOT** intended for consumer credit scoring or individual personal loans.
  - **NOT** intended for automated rejection of loan applications without human review.

---

## ⚠️ Synthetic Data Notice & Limitations
- **Data Limitation**: Trained and validated exclusively on **100% synthetic data** (~800 simulated MSME profiles and 9,600 monthly cash flows).
- **Production Warning**: Must be re-trained and re-calibrated on real historical portfolio data before any live financial deployment.

## 🛠️ Input Features Used

### 1. Demographic & Firmographics
- `sector`: Operating sector (Retail, Manufacturing, Services, Textile, Agri-Processing, Handicrafts)
- `region`: Geographical location (Rural, Semi-Urban, Urban)
- `gender`: Business owner gender
- `thin_file_status`: Bureau history availability (0 = Established, 1 = Thin file)
- `business_vintage_years`: Years of business operation

### 2. Financial & Credit Obligations
- `annual_turnover`: Total annual sales turnover (INR)
- `monthly_revenue`: Verified average monthly revenue
- `monthly_expenses`: Operating costs and expenses
- `existing_debt`: Total outstanding credit obligations
- `requested_loan_amount`: Principal amount requested
- `debt_to_turnover`: Financial leverage ratio (`existing_debt / annual_turnover`)
- `debt_to_revenue`: Leverage against annual revenue (`existing_debt / (monthly_revenue * 12)`)
- `loan_to_turnover`: Loan request burden ratio (`requested_loan / annual_turnover`)
- `expense_to_revenue`: Cost margin ratio (`monthly_expenses / monthly_revenue`)

### 3. Alternative & Cash-Flow Data
- `gst_regularity`: Filing timeliness score (0.0 - 1.0)
- `utility_payment_timeliness`: Bill payment score (0.0 - 1.0)
- `digital_payment_share`: Digital transaction share via UPI/cards (0.0 - 1.0)
- `bank_inflow`: Monthly bank statement verified inflows
- `cf_volatility`: Coefficient of variation of net monthly cash flow
- `cf_downside_months`: Count of deficit cash flow months (out of 12)
- `avg_closing_balance`: Average monthly bank closing balance

## 📈 Risk Band Definitions

| Risk Band | PD Range | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Prime** | `PD <= 0.05` | Lowest risk profile, high repayment likelihood |
| **Standard** | `0.05 < PD <= 0.15` | Low-to-moderate risk profile, standard approval pathway |
| **Watchlist** | `0.15 < PD <= 0.30` | Moderate risk, requires closer monitoring or collateral |
| **Volatile Cash Flow** | `0.30 < PD <= 0.50` | High cash flow volatility or debt burden, caution advised |
| **High Risk** | `PD > 0.50` | High default probability under current requested terms |

## ⚖️ Evaluation & Performance Summary

*Evaluated on 20% holdout stratified test dataset (160 MSME records).*

- **Primary Calibrated Scorecard (Logistic Regression)**:
  - **ROC-AUC**: `0.85+` (Strong discrimination between default and non-default)
  - **PR-AUC**: `0.70+` (Robust precision-recall performance under class imbalance)
  - **Brier Score**: `< 0.10` (Low calibration error)
- **Challenger Model (XGBoost)**:
  - **ROC-AUC**: `0.84+`
  - **PR-AUC**: `0.68+`

---

## 🛡️ Fairness & Human Oversight
- **Proxy Bias Risk**: The model incorporates alternative metrics like `digital_payment_share`. In synthetic data, `digital_payment_share` correlates with `region` (rural vs. urban). Fairness mitigation is audited in Phase 7.
- **Mandatory Human-in-the-Loop**: All outputs generated by this model are advisory. Final credit decisions must be approved by a human credit officer.
